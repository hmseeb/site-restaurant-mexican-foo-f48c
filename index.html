Catering & large orders

Birthdays, graduations, quinceañeras, church events, office lunches and family get-togethers. Tell us your headcount and your date and we’ll put together a plan for trays, sides and salsas so there is enough for everyone.

Catering requests get a same-day reply during opening hours

  1. 01

    Dine in and eat it the way it’s meant to be served

    Hot plates, straight from the kitchen to your table. Bring the family, bring a group, or come in on your own — let us know if you’re arriving with a big party so we can set the room up for you.

  2. 02

    Order takeout and have dinner handled

    Call-ahead and pick-up orders for nights when nobody wants to cook. Send your order through the form and we’ll confirm what you asked for and when it will be ready.

  3. 03

    Family-style meals for the whole table

    Bigger portions meant to be shared and passed around, so a household or a group of friends can eat together from the same spread instead of ordering plate by plate.

  4. 04

    Group bookings for your table

    Planning to come in with a crowd? Send us the date, the time and how many people, and we’ll get back to you about seating your group.
